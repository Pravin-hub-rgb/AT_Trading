## What the walk shows that the totals hide

1. **More blocks lose than win, in both eras.** IS 23/27, OOS 11/17. The book
   profits from payoff asymmetry, not hit rate. Any "consistent +325.8R" reading
   is wrong.

2. **The OOS sat underwater for 15 straight blocks** — trades 51-170, May 2021
   to Feb 2022, `cum` never once exceeding −2.6R. That is 120 trades and ~9
   months of continuous decline. **The entire +70.2R is recovered in the back
   half.** Anyone starting in 2021 would have quit.

3. **Two blocks carry 131.2% of OOS.** 201-210 (+31.4, May–Jun 2022) and 261-270
   (+60.7, Oct–Nov 2022) produce more than the whole net. Everything else sums
   to −21.9R.

4. **Deepest point is −32.7R** (OOS 161-170). Worth noting this is *larger* than
   the IS drawdown of 27.7 — the deep DD is not caused by long losing blocks, it
   is caused by the gaps between them.

5. **Blocks 311-320 and 401-410 are 0/10 wins** — ten straight losses, −11.5R
   and −10.9R. With a ~1R stop that is the arithmetic floor, and it happened
   twice in 3.7 years.

6. **Best single trades +48.5, +54.8, +42.3, +35.7, +32.6 against a worst of
   −3.4.** Roughly 15:1 at the extreme. That is what the whole book is built on.

## What to do with this

The walk is the operational argument for the position-sizing question that
`method/IS_IT_RIGHT.md` and `method/TAIL_RISK_AUDIT.md` both reached from other directions. A
book where 27 of 50 blocks are negative, where 15 straight blocks can sit 30R
underwater, and where two blocks carry 131% of out-of-sample profit, cannot be
sized as though it has a steady edge. It has to be sized so a −33R block does
not matter.
