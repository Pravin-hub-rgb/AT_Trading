# Experiment harness index — 2026-10-15

Every harness run on the corrected swing chain in this session, what it asked,
what it answered, and how to re-run it. All of these are committed; nothing here
lives only on disk.

All of them are identity-gated and measure both eras. None of them promotes a
book by itself.

## Two mistakes worth not repeating

**A gate that selects for luck.** The first selection gate was "no negative
50-trade rolling window in OOS". It rejected all three books the other bot had
just registered, at `−5.57R`, `−5.85R` and `−1.53R` on books making 260–328R.
Their rolling windows were 96.9%, 98.3% and 96.5% positive — better than most
books that passed. The gate was not a quality filter, it was a luck filter, and
replacing it with the share of positive windows took multi admissions from 10
to 29.

**A number I could not cite.** `DD_BUDGET_PCT = 5.0` was mine, not from any
document — picked by looking at where the distribution happened to fall. It is
gone, and so is the fixed 29R it replaced. There is no drawdown ceiling now,
because a ceiling cannot tell a big book from a small one: it rejected a 2.7%
drawdown on a book that made 765.9R and passed a 39.4% one on a book that made
35.7R.
