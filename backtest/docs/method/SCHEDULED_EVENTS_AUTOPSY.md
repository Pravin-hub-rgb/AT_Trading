# Stage 22 — Official Scheduled-Event Autopsy

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Scope:** BTCUSDT Bybit perpetual only  
**References:** fullstack wage10/cd0 single, champion multi, UTC-Saturday multi  
**Periods:** IS 2023–2026 and reused OOS 2021–2022

## Question

Should new v18 entries be suppressed around publicly scheduled U.S. macro events that were knowable before they occurred?

## Calendar integrity

The calendar is generated by `scripts/build_scheduled_event_calendar.py` and stored at `data/scheduled_events/official_us_events_2021_2026.json`.

- 290 BLS exact-time releases: CPI, Employment Situation/NFP, PPI, and JOLTS.
- 46 FOMC exact-time monetary-policy statements.
- 46 BEA exact-time PCE/GDP events from the current official API, retained only as a supplemental 2025–2026 family.
- 4 fixed political dates, stored as date-only and never used in minute windows.
- 54 official source records with URLs, SHA-256 hashes, and conversion rules.

No event is included because its market reaction was bearish/bullish. No surprise announcement, hack, liquidation, ETF decision, tariff, or unverified Grok narrative is removed. Exact-time events require an official scheduled timestamp. The candidate entry instant is `signal candle timestamp + 900 seconds`, preventing a 15-minute timestamp leak.

## Descriptive coverage

Executed trades intersecting the exact window were sparse:

- Single: 3 IS and 3 OOS rows.
- Champion multi: 14 IS and 7 OOS rows.
- UTC-Saturday multi: 15 IS and 8 OOS rows.

Most multi IS event-window trades were negative, but the samples are too small for a universal conclusion. FOMC intersected only one OOS king trade. The current BEA API contributed only three IS multi rows and therefore was not used as a cross-era gate.

## Path-true policies

Frozen policies:

1. Block new candidates from 60 minutes before through 30 minutes after official BLS/FOMC events.
2. Block new candidates on the entire UTC event day.
3. Block fixed political dates.
4. Date-shift placebos: official core calendar minus one day and plus one day, tested on both multi references.

The gate blocks new entries only. Existing positions retain their normal structural exits. Later eligible signals can refill the portfolio, so blocked-candidate count is not equal to final trade-count reduction.

## Interpretation

The exact window is not robust:

- Single changes are only +1.0R IS and +1.1R OOS.
- Champion is +7.3R IS but −12.0R OOS.
- King is −6.2R IS but +15.4R OOS.

The full UTC day is harmful in every IS reference: −82.7R single, −18.7R champion, and −45.6R king. The king's +29.5R OOS result is regime-specific rather than cross-era.

Political dates have no meaningful universal effect; IS is unchanged and OOS changes are only +0.2R to +1.4R.

The shifted placebos are important. On champion, the −1d shifted calendar improves both IS (+32.0R) and OOS (+5.8R), while the true exact window hurts OOS. This is a date-mining warning rather than clean evidence for the official calendar.

## Verdict

The hypothesis is **not supported as a universal strategy improvement**.

- No event-direction gate is possible without hindsight labels.
- The narrow exact window has inconsistent configuration/era behavior and negligible single-lane effect.
- The full-day policy removes valuable later opportunities and damages IS.
- Sparse event-hit samples and favorable shifted placebos make the apparent king OOS gain untrustworthy as a general edge.

No production change is authorized. The official calendar builder, source ledger, test-only gate, and result artifacts are retained for future event-family work; no further threshold or date-offset sweep is justified from this evidence.
