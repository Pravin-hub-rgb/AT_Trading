# MULTI PLANE - two rankings

Generated from `backtest/tests/_multi_metrics.json` by
`backtest/tests/_gen_multi_rankings_doc.py`. Multi plane only
(`max_positions != 1`), both eras, quarantined look-ahead books excluded,
books under 30 trades in either era excluded.

`n` counts CLOSED trades. The registry's net pins exclude positions still
open at the era boundary (their r is mark-to-market, not realized), so a
book's `n` here can sit under the board's n while `net` matches its pin -
`n_open` in the source JSON holds that difference.

## Why two lists

| ranking | favours | hides |
|---|---|---|
| **by total net R** | books that run many slots and trade often | how good each trade was |
| **by R per trade** | books whose trades are worth more | how many there were |

Both are correct and they disagree, which is the point. On this plane the
total is partly bought with concurrency: `max_positions` is not 1, so a
book can raise total R by overlapping trades without any trade being
better. R per trade is the only one of the two that cannot be raised
that way.
