Before archiving, v19_champion.py was checked against unified.py on all three
pivot rules, trade for trade including exits, R and stop price: 500/+445.22
origin, 499/+355.72 leg, 500/+445.19 leg_no_entry, IDENTICAL in every case.

The v19_* route keys now resolve to unified.py through an alias, so the dropdown
entries keep working while pointing at one engine.

NEW BOOK - CHAMPION ENTRY-1 (pivot_rule='leg_no_entry', version
'entry_minus_1'). pivot_rule='leg' reads the extreme from the swing start up
to and including the break bar, so the entry bar own extreme can set the
stop. 'leg_no_entry' stops one bar earlier. Both are causal - both read
only bars that closed before the entry.

It is worth stating what the measurements show, because it cuts against the
usual framing: leg_no_entry lands within 0.03R of the origin book over 500
trades, with 497 of 500 stops identical. So the origin pivot is not
measurably lookahead. What C16 changed was the risk denominator, not
knowledge of the future. C16's rule is still the right one to ship - the
structural extreme rather than a structural pivot - but 'lookahead'
overstated it. Recorded in the registry beside the three books.

One regression caught on the way: the v19_* branches relied on max_positions and
exp_persist being hardcoded inside v19_champion.py. unified.py takes them as
arguments, so a branch that omitted them got the neutral default and
v19_champion returned 503 trades / +443.58R against its 500 / +445.22R pin.
All three branches now pass them explicitly. Re-verified after the fix:
