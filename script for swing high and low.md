**Haan, TradingView pe kaafi useful free/open-source scripts hain** jo swing structure + major Support/Resistance detect karte hain. Ayush style (swing domination + structure) ke liye ye best fit karte hain.

### Best Scripts for Your System (Swing + Major S/R)

#### 1. Swing High / Low Detectors (Most Important for you)
| Script Name | Author | Key Feature | Link Style |
|-------------|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Swing Highs and Lows Detector** | The_Forex_Steward | HH / HL / LH / LL labels + structure shifts | Search: "Swing Highs and Lows Detector" |
| **Swing High/Low (Adaptive)** | dchunt-stack | Adaptive invalidation (purane weak swings automatically remove) | Search same name |
| **Swing High Low Detector by RV5** | R_V5 | Current + past swings, lines extend till broken | Very clean |
| **Swing High/Low Support Resistance** | darshakssc | Simple pivot based S/R from swings | Good starting point |

Yeh scripts aapke **Zone system** ke liye perfect hain — major swing highs = Resistance candidates, swing lows = Support candidates.

#### 2. Market Structure Scripts (HH/HL/LH/LL + Bias)
- **Market Structure** by tgregg75 → Buy/Sell signals on structure break + background bias
- **Price Action Structure** by ADXAE → Internal + Swing structure (BOS / CHoCH)
- **Swing Structure Forecast [BOSWaves]** → Swing based zones + statistical projection

#### 3. Auto Support & Resistance Zones (Better than single lines)
- **Auto Support and Resistance with Swing Pivots + Touch Strength** → Touch count dikhata hai (kitni baar test hua)
- **Support/Resistance Auto Draw [bolleta]** → Zones + role reversal (S becomes R)
- **Auto Support/Resistance Zones** by ADXAE → Clusters nearby pivots into proper zones

#### 4. Volume Profile (Ayush volume theory ke close)
TradingView built-in:
- **Volume Profile Visible Range (VPVR)**
- **Fixed Range Volume Profile**
- **Periodic Volume Profile**

POC (Point of Control), VAH, VAL automatically major levels ban jaate hain.

### Recommendation for Your Coding Project

**Best combination for overview:**

1. **Swing Highs and Lows Detector** (or Adaptive version) → Major swings identify karne ke liye
2. **Auto Support/Resistance with Touch Strength** → Strong zones filter karne ke liye
3. **Volume Profile** → Volume confirmation ke liye

In scripts se aap:
- Major Resistance / Support zones nikaal sakte ho
- Current price kis zone mein hai (1 to 5) decide kar sakte ho
- Previous swing structure se domination (buyer/seller) samajh sakte ho

### Practical Tip
TradingView pe search karte time ye keywords use karo:
- `"Swing High Low"`
- `"Market Structure"`
- `"Auto Support Resistance"`
- `"Pivot Support Resistance"`

Open-source wale scripts prefer karo (code dekh sakte ho, modify bhi kar sakte ho).
