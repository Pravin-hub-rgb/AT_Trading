run, which is the wrong way to answer that question - the ranking should come out

The important part is that the useful dates are NOT the membership ones.
488 of 500 trades exist in both books. Looking at 2023-10-05 or 2024-12-28
shows a trade appearing or vanishing, which is a real difference but a small one,
and it does not show what produces the 77.82R.
The gap is stop placement on trades both books take:
  trades only in RETRO        +4.40R  (12)
  trades only in CAUSAL       -5.99R  (6)
  R change on shared trades   -78.79R  (316)
  identical                    0.00R  (172)
So the dates to open are the ones in section A, where the same trade has a
visibly different stop.
