One thing to be clear about: the chart draws ONE chain, the one for the
timeframe on screen. The 1h leg at min_bars=10 is used inside the engine to decide
which windows are open, but it is not drawn. So the B2/FLIP marks you can see are
the 15m ones, and the 1h window that gates each entry is invisible. That is why the
09:15 signal existed with no visible 1h window around it to explain the 12:15
entry. Not a bug, but it is why the question was hard to answer from the screen
alone.
