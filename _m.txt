Asked why no mark appeared at 2024-11-19 09:15 when the engine had a B2 signal
there. The engine says:

  2024-11-19 06:45  B2  dir=+1  swing#1468   <- the trade, entered 12:15
  2024-11-19 09:15  B2  dir=-1  swing#1469   <- a second signal, gated out

both of which do have marks. But the chart showed nothing near either, because
the chart and the engine were not looking at the same swings:

  chart  strategyChainMinBars -> btParams.minBars -> BT_DEFAULTS + masterM.preset
          = 20   (the #1 Flip master preset carries minBars: 20)
  engine bookSizing.minBars = 50

minBars 20 and minBars 50 produce different swing chains. Different chains
means different pivot locations, so every mark on screen sits somewhere the engine
never looked. Not close to right - structurally unrelated.

Two changes:
  strategyChainMinBars is now bookSizing.minBars, full stop. The old value had
    a second rule keyed on htfLtf that could override the book - a second thing
    that can disagree, which is the whole problem this commit is about.
  btParams no longer merges masterM.preset. Master presets are recorded sweeps,
    not books; merging one in is what put minBars=20 there in the first place.
    btParams now carries the cost model and nothing else.
exp_entry_why.py added, which prints the mark stream, the derived signals and the
actual entries for any date window, so this chain is checkable rather than
inferred. It also shows the answer to the earlier question: entries fire on B2 or
FLIP, whichever comes first, and a signal can sit for hours before the 1h window
gate lets it in. The 09:15 signal existed; the gate opened at 12:15 and a different
signal (06:45, swing 1468, long) was the one that got in.
