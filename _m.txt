bookSizing now survives a reload; that is what turned 500 into 1052
The funding change did not move the trade count. The reload did.
bookSizing is not persisted to localStorage, and its useState initialiser was
the literal {minBars: 14, htfTf: 1h, htfMinBars: 10}. The mount effect restored a saved
book - version, tf, symbol, htfLtf, htfLtfVer - but never called setBookSizing.
So after any reload:
  version   = v19_champion  (from the book)
  minBars   = 14            (from the stale literal)
which is a combination no book in books.ts describes. At minBars 14 the 15m swing
chain is roughly twice as long, so the signal stream and the trail ratchet both
behave differently: 1052 trades, TRAIL 836, SL 216, DD 28.14R.
Fixed in two places:
  the useState initialiser now reads boot.config (the default book) instead of
    the literal 14
  the savedBook branch of the mount effect now calls setBookSizing, which it
    never did. Only the no-saved-book branch had it, which is why a first visit
    worked and every reload after broke.
This is the second time a state initialiser has quietly outvoted the book.
