## The challenges with quick, momentum-based execution

To trade like this, a few things must be true:

1. **Your execution has to be quick.** Timing matters. If you are slow, the risk management won't hold.
2. **Don't enter before the momentum** actually starts. If you enter too early (in the "parked"), the market may not move — the stop-loss gets hit. Entering early is not a *safe* entry.
3. **The professional entry is "getting on a moving train and getting off a moving train."** You enter while the market moves in the momentum — and exit while it's still moving. If you sit in a "parked car" (sideways), nothing happens and you are better off staying out.

So the ideal entry is one where the market supplies the momentum right after you enter. The stop is small, so **the momentum has to come quickly** to keep the trade safe.

## Average candle size & realistic targets

Because the stop is small, the target must line up with the session. The basis is the **average candle size** — the average range/height of the session's candles:

- Measure the average candle size (sum highs-lows / number of candles), or estimate by the general candle size and take a slightly larger-than-average reference.
- Since the SL is placed very near the low, the base criterion for expected momentum is at least one average candle / a few points of move. Because the SL is small, an entry at a good spot sees the market jump 5-10 points quickly — that jump makes the SL automatically safe.
- **Don't set the SL too big** — then the target ratio becomes big and the entry gets sloppy, and in many cases the target is missed. Keep the SL small and the target realistic.

### The 5-point immediate-momentum rule (most important point)
"average size का SL plan करेंगे... यह entry तो इसमें 5 point का momentum आ गया है suddenly entry के बाद तो ऐसा maximum case में होना चीए यह most important point" — **After your entry, roughly 5 points of momentum should arrive almost immediately.** If it does, the small SL is instantly protected. This is THE most important checkpoint of this system. If that quick push doesn't come, the entry needs improvement.

### The key entry rule
Small SL + small target = the entry must be precise. Without immediate follow-through, you can't run a small-SL, small-target system.

## Two execution scenarios: flow-favor vs flow-against

This is today's core structural distinction:

### Scenario A — Execution IN favor of the flow
"direction के favor में execution कर रहे है तो हम pace of momentum के break पर direct जैसे ही support identification होगा we will execute on to the buying side... हम वहाँ पर N और M यह कुछ नहीं देखेंगे हम pace of momentum का break देखेंगे break आया है we will execute" — When the market dips WITHIN your favorable direction (e.g., buying trend, market retraces): **no N/M check needed.** As soon as support identifies via the pace-of-momentum break, execute directly. Simple.

### Scenario B — Execution AGAINST the flow
"flow के favor में नहीं है retracement awaited है तो यहां आप क्या कर रहो buying market चलता है हम sell कर रहे हैं और combination देखेगा" — When selling INTO a buying flow (retracement expected from higher levels): this needs **confirmation entry** — minimum **two rejections**, with execution possible on the third. Because in a buying flow, once a buying swing forms, continuous reversal won't happen — so domination must be proven first.

### Morning-session preference
"ज़्यादा preferable entry morning का होता है मेरा personal... क्योंकि momentum morning में आएगा और maximum time अच्छा momentum मिलता है तो morning hours preferable" — He personally prefers morning-hour entries because that's where the best momentum lives. You can also trade per the overview later ("continuous नहीं गिरेगा, जहां support बनाएगा bounce back आएगा"), but the visible morning opportunity is itself high-probability: weakness building → minimum expectation = intraday-low breakdown → execute.

## Timing rules

- **No entry before 9:45.** "9.45 के बाद entry होगा... I am not recommending anyone कि आप किसी भी case में execution करें छोड़ दीजे वो case but 9.45 के बाद ही आपका entry plan होगा" — Skip any setup that appears before 9:45. Standard rule.
- **Avoid entries after ~11:12.** "11.12 बजे के बाद entry को avoid करें अगर morning session trade करें... max 12 के बाद तो नहीं लेना बाकि उसके पहले ले सकते हैं" — For morning-session trades, avoid entries after ~11:12; absolutely nothing after 12.
- **10 AM = the formation window.** "आप आँख बंद करके अगर data भी उठा करके देखो कि तो आप देखो कि जो swing बन रहे हैं ये 10 बजे के आसपास ही market का पहला swing का या support या resistance बन रहा है... वैसे ही जो timing है वो एक high probability timing है" — Even picking charts blind, the first swing's support/resistance forms around 10 AM. Multiple dated examples shown (March 1, late-Feb days) all form their first S/R near the 10 o'clock candle. **Timing is crucial** — keep it at the back of your mind: the level isn't formed yet; it will form in a couple of candles, maybe after breaking lows first — but it WILL form there.

### Why the market behaves this way
"market का nature है कि open होने के बाद उस आज़ादा 5 candle 8 candle 10 candle एक direction में जाएगा और resist करेगा उसके बाद फिर direction planning करेगा तो 5-6-8-10 candle का timing क्या बन रहा है 5 minute के according 10 बजे के round बन रहा है 9.45 के बाद बन रहा है तो logic समझ रहा है हम market के according चीज़ों को adapt कर रहा है अपने according market को नहीं चला रहा है" — After open, the market runs 5–8–10 candles one way, then resists, then plans direction. Five-minute candles × 8–10 ≈ the 10 o'clock zone. We ADAPT ourselves to the market's rhythm — we don't drag the market to our schedule. If every day opened with huge unidirectional swings, everyone would short every dip and buy every rise — the market can't function that way, so uncertainty comes first, then the decisive flow.

## The first swing is never traded inside

"आप जो मेरा maximum entry देखेंगे उसमें यहीं देखेंगे पहला swing है मैं उसके continuation के direction में execute नहीं करता हूँ maximum number of time आपको यहीं देखने को मिलेगा पहला swing complete होगा इसके बाद मैं या तो उसके favor में continuation लूँगा या फिर तो reversal लूँगा पहले ही swing में घुसकर के गिर रहा है वैसे नहीं क्योंकि वो 4-5-6 candle ही जाएगा maximum cases में" — **He does NOT enter inside the first swing.** Wait for it to complete (it rarely exceeds 5–6 candles; max ~8–10), THEN take either its continuation or its reversal. Entering mid-first-swing is how people get chopped.

"maximum cases जो देखें हैं क्या उसमें पहले ही swing से बहुत बड़ा-बड़ा continuation होता है क्या yes or no — नहीं होता है... एक दो case exceptional create होंगे कि पहले ही swing से selling आने लगा और गिरता चला गया first of March का दिन था buying आया बढ़ता चला गया बट भो भी तो resist किया न" — Does the first swing ever continue hugely? Generally NO — 1–2 exceptional days aside (and even March 1's runner resisted along the way). Back-of-mind: the market WILL resist; that certainty is your edge.

## Pace of momentum break — the identification engine

### Definition
"pace of momentum का break observe करना easy है simple है हम simple जो भी swing market पहला create कर रहा है either selling side है either buying side है तो swing में किस तरह का selling buying activity हुआ है इसको observe किया जाता है... जिस pattern का selling है या जिस pattern का buying है उसका break जहां से observe होगा कि कुछ अब unusual हो रहा है... तो उसको pace of momentum का break कहा जाता है... अगर break हुआ है तो जादा probability वहाँ पर support बनने वाला" — Observe the NATURE of the first swing (how its selling/buying activity behaves). Wherever that established pattern turns *unusual*, that's the pace-of-momentum break — and the highest-probability location for support/resistance formation.

### THREE types of pattern breaks
"पैसा ऑफ मोमेंटम का break दो तरीके से create होता है एक तो होगा कि market जो general pattern है वो candle wise break करेगा... और दूसरा होगा कि domination wise break करेगा... और तीसरा है candle size से break करेगा aggressive nature होगा यह तीन चीज़े हो सकते"

1. **Candle-wise pattern break:** the established up/down rhythm breaks (e.g., alternating 1-green-1-red suddenly becomes 3 consecutive selling candles).
2. **Domination-wise break:** an aggressive domination candle consumes/reverses the swing sharply.
3. **Candle-size/aggressive break:** a candle far larger than the session's average appears — unusual size itself signals the break.

### Reading the swing rhythm lets you predict the next candle
"इस buying swing में किस तरह का momentum हो रहा है तीन दो candle उपर गया एक candle rejection आया seller एक candle में dominate किया इसके बाद क्या होगा again buyers ने high break कर दिया तो अभी तक का observation morning में वो क्या होगा कि एक candle आ सकता है फिर normal है कि एक seller आयेगा और buyer continuation कर देगा... तो हमें पहले से पता एक candle आयेगा buyer again उपर की तरफ ले जा सकता है" — If the swing's rhythm is "2–3 green, 1 red rejection, greens resume," then you KNOW the next red will likely be absorbed and highs broken again. No action on single reds — the pattern hasn't broken. The break arrives only via: 2–3 sideways candles / lows breaking after a high, OR one big domination red. Until then: wait.

### Confirmation needs follow-through
"आयन कोई भी consume नहीं किया... इसके बाद कोई भी अगला candle में continuation नहीं हुआ तो इसमें और confirmation को wait करना है... wait करके जब again continuation होने लगा तो इतना बड़ा selling market reverse कर रहा है support के बाद एक domination candle close भी हुआ और again पहली बार ऐसा हो रहा है कि उपर और continuation हो रहा है तो it's a confirmation" — One big candle alone isn't a confirmed break; you need the NEXT candles to continue in the new direction. Big candle + close + follow-through continuation = confirmed pattern break.

### First break = highest probability spot
"flow के break को observe करना है तो ये break पहली बार यहां पर आया है तो जहां भी break आएगा वहीं सबसे ज़ादा probability है कि support बनने वाला है या resistance बनने वाला है" — Wherever the FIRST break appears is where S/R most probably forms.

### Market nature is the only certainty — hence universal
"आप किस चीज़ पर market में sure हो सकते है ऐसी कोई चीज़ नहीं जिस पर आप market में sure होके कह सकते हैं कि ऐसा होगा ना सिवाए किस चीज़ के market nature के कि one direction नहीं जा सकता है major overview पर भी छोटे से छोटे time frame पर भी और intraday के first swing में भी... maximum time exceptional days can be there बट वो market का nature नहीं है तो nature को ही use किया जा रहा है हर एक case में तभी concept universal" — Nothing in markets is certain EXCEPT the nature: price cannot travel one direction forever. That holds on every timeframe including the intraday first swing. Trading THAT nature is what makes the concept universal.

### Point-to-point identical identification
"ऐसा किसी के साथ नहीं होगा कि अगर कोई rule follow कर रहा है तो कोई member का ऐसा नहीं होगा किसी ने पहले identify कर ले ना यहां आया है मतलब यहीं आया है इसमें कोई confusion नहीं ऐसा है point to point" — Follow the rules and everyone identifies the SAME point. No ambiguity.

## The two types of entries

There are two entry types:

1. **Type 1 (easy to identify):** the market makes a high, and then the selling domination forms **below** the high — creating a lower-high / lower-low pattern. Once that low breaks with domination, you act.
2. **Type 2:** the high breaks one by one (only one-two candles against), but the domination is on the selling side — a slower, weaker-pace case where you need the confirmation from a clean pattern-break candle.

In swing cases previously discussed, when "weakness" is forming but the market keeps making one-candle highs, ultimately the domination resolves one way.

---

## Confirmation via rejection & "third rejection"

- When the market approaches and **rejects** a level repeatedly (signals of selling/weakness), the confirmation builds after proper domination and rejection.
- **Two rejections minimum; execute on the third:** "दो rejection necessary है तीसरे rejection पर execute कर सकता है"
- **What the third rejection confirms:** "तीसरा rejection क्या confirmation है तीसरा rejection का ही यही confirmation है कि जो previous high अगर उस तक market नहीं जा पा रहा है उसके पहले ही गिरने लग रहा है तो it's a confirmation कि rejection आना start होगे और तीसरा rejection होगा तो उसका भी फिर again अपोस्ट करना probability कम हो जाता है" — The third rejection proves the market can't even reach the prior high before rolling over; the probability of opposing IT also collapses. That's the entry trigger.
- **If no third rejection comes:** "third rejection नहीं होता तो domination के confirmation पर बनता कि यह clear cut pattern break का candle है... momentum जब आगी जाए वो confirmation है अपने आप में self confirmation है price का" — Entry then forms on the clean pattern-break/domination candle; arriving momentum is itself price's self-confirmation.
- If the overview is selling and the market tested a major low (e.g., March 1), that's **against** the selling direction and in the trader's favour — so a direct call is allowed WITHOUT waiting for three rejections: "first March को major lowest test हुआ था so overview was of selling major तो वो against the direction था तो हमारे favor में था न selling में ही था support test हुआ है तो इसलिए directly किया गया three time rejection नहीं देखा गया" — But when selling AT higher levels after a major-high test, the three-rejection check applies.

**Meta-rule:** once you're in a trade, **follow your plan** — don't change it mid-trade. If you've decided "no action before 1:2 / exit after 1:3," do exactly that, even if price runs past and then reverses.

## Volume & option-strike confirmation

### General volume signature of a session
"opening की जो एक दो general candles होती है उसमें volume आता है ये nature of markets हैं... market जब open होता है तब एक spike आता है ऐसा फिर side-hays हो जाता है और फिर closing के time पर spike आता है ये general nature of volumes है... morning के बाद जो एक reduction होता है उसका break आपको देखने को मिलेगा जहां भी आजसा domination होने वाला है maximum case में काफी सारे cases में देखने को मिलेगा" — Volume spikes at open → reduces through mid-session → **the BREAK of that reduction marks where domination occurs** → spike again at close. Watch for the mid-day volume-break.

- When the market falls, a **significant/double volume increase** in the option is a confirmation of a confident move: "general जहाँ domination आएगा proper वहाँ double volume देखने को मिलेगा"
- If the market falls but **volume does not increase**, it's a **divergence / not a confident move**: "market नीचे गिर रहा हो बट volume ना increase हो रहा हो तो समझ जाए उस chase में उतना confident move नहीं है वो... तो उसमें थोड़ा और confirmation लेकरके execute करना है"
- **Big volume ON the support-identification candle is itself a confirmation:** "इतने बड़ा volume बाहर आ जाएगा... जब यह support identification candle है इसमें create हो जाएगा तो it's again a confirmation in itself"

### Divergence reading
"divergence मतलब option chart पर spot के chart पर तो highs break हो रहा है option पर नहीं हो रहा है यह option पर और नीचे lows पर आगया और जादा weakness है" — Spot makes highs but the option doesn't follow; or the option sags to lows while spot holds — extra weakness/strength information. Option divergence appearing BEFORE spot reverses lets you "execute even more confidently."

### Which chart for volume?
"options देखते है volumes के लिए future नहीं देखेंगे option में execute करेंगे तो option देखेंगे generally stocks के लिए future का chart देखा जाता है... लेकिन nifty में हम option के strike का ही volume देखते हैं" — Executing in Nifty options → watch the OPTION STRIKE's own volume (futures optional). Stock options → futures volume is the norm. Futures chart remains usable for general direction; both may be watched — personal choice.

### Strike selection via volume break
"volume में एक आपको break observe होगा जो general volume चल रहा है वो volume से जो general candles उसका double volume देखने को मिलेगा... जिसमें आपको volume observe हो जादा वो strike जादा preferable होगा और आप maximum time देखोगे कि जिस strike में divergence है जिस strike में जादा strength है उसी में volume break देखने को मिलेगा हर एक strike में नहीं मिलेगा add the money in the money एक दो तीन strike आप check करोगे तो ऐसा कई cases में होगा कि एक दो strike में तो आया है बाकी में नहीं आया है volumes... तो जिसमें volume break आएगा वही strike ज्यादा strong होगा उसमें एक अलग divergence देखने को मिलेगा" — Check ATM + 1–2 ITM strikes: usually only ONE shows the volume break/divergence. **That strike is the strongest — prefer it.**

## Risk management — reversal rules

- **Momentum beyond 1:2 then reversing → exit early or at cost:** "अगर 1 ratio 2 से ज़्यादा momentum कर गया है तो reverse होता है तो कोशिश करने है कि trade को पहले exit कर दें अगर reversal आता है या SL ना दे cost to cost exit कर दे" 
- **Missing 1:3 and reversing → don't panic-exit at the top; cut near cost:** "target point 1 ratio 3 कुछ momentum से miss हो रहा है और reverse हो रहा है तो ऐसा नहीं जल्द बाजिया पहले exit कर जब near the cost आने लगा है तो cut दीजे यह risk management का rule हो गया"
- **Cost-to-cost exit unlocks a second trade:** "दूसरा trade ले सकते हैं अगर cost to cost entry है exit है तो दूसरा trade execute कर सकते है again direction planning कर के"

### Advanced techniques — NOT advised yet
- **Early exit on weak reversal (before 1:2):** "अगर one ratio two के पहली reverse होता है... अभी advise नहीं है आपको कि आप जब perfection हो जाएगा जब उतनी चीजें strong confirmations है कि market में यह तो ऐसल ले जाने ही वाला है तो उस case में पहले ही exit कर दिया जाता है but अभी beginning में यह चीजें advise नहीं है" — Requires strong confirmations + perfection. Premature exits that get followed by the intended move **disrupt the mindset** ("exit मार दिया और फिर पता चला वहां से नीचे गिर गया") — avoid in the beginning.
- **Support-break cut-and-re-enter:** "support बनने पर entry ले लिया bounce back आ रहा है और इसी base पर मैं कुछ दिन ऐसा कर पाता हूं कि bounce back आवा resist करके अगर reversal successful sufficient हो रहा है entry अच्छे point को है तो वहीं cut देंगे उसको कि ये तो support break करने आदा और break कर देगा और नया support बनाएगा हम फिर ले लेंगे" — With experience: if the post-entry bounce resists and a support break looks imminent from a good entry, cut early and re-enter at the NEW support. Skill-based; not for beginners.

## Order types & exits

- **Entry orders:** "limit order को ही market order की तरह थोड़ा use कर सकते है... limit और stop loss and stop loss limit order SLM use होता है... अगर buy करना है तो आप higher price का limit लगा कर सकते होगे इतना price cross करेगा तो buy करेंगे या limit लगा सकते है कि इस price के नीचे ही नीचे हम entry orders मिल जाए" — Use LIMIT creatively (buy-stop-style limit above price, or bargain limit below). ## Retracement-expectation logic (the short case)

"एक general retracement was expected कि market continuous जा रहा है support बनाने के बाद continuous lower level से highs की तरह जा रहा है तो continuous जाएगा तो क्या होता है वहाँ पर एक retracement expected होता है... 10 बजे के आसपास कहीं से जो pattern break आएगा वो pattern break में market क्या करेगा resist करेगा और resist करेगा तो retrace करेगा... minimum इतना retracement expected था कि at least support break करके थोड़ा बहुत तो retrace करेगा and retrace करेगा तो उतने में या तो अगर reverse होगा तो cost to cost exit कर लेंगे और otherwise वहाँ पर दूसरा हम trade plan कर लेंगे" — After a strong extended rally from lows: expect retracement around the 10-o'clock pattern break; minimum expectation = support break + some pullback; plan either the cost-to-cost exit or the second trade from there.

### Big swings shrink reversal probability
"strength के swing वाला भी case है कि market continuously काफी बड़ा swing है higher lows hold किया है retracement आया बट swing का size काफी जादा बड़ा हो गया तो जब भी one direction काफी बड़ा अगर momentum आ जाए तो उसके फिर again lows तक reverse होने का probability reduce हो जाता है selling domination होने का probability automatically reduce हो जाता है" — The bigger the completed swing, the lower the odds of full reversal back to lows. Size tempers expectations.
