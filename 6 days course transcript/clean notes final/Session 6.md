### Why exit: the three-attempts rule
Market proceeded AGAINST the short toward the same level THREE times. Each buyer attempt knocked down by sellers is itself buyer-weakness; odds of an against-flow move keep rising. General rule: reversing near your target after repeated attempts means only that much momentum ever existed (entry ~140; booking near/below ~180 region sufficed) → third reversal forming = exit cost-to-cost. These few simple rules ARE risk management, discipline, execution combined.

## The professional entry (recap + depth)

Motive of every entry: form it WHEN MOMENTUM STARTS — then some momentum arrives and the SL becomes automatically safe.

- After entry, **5–10 points favorable should come almost immediately**; with a small SL expect ~1:1-worth momentum instantly (same or next 1–2 candles) = perfect entry. Today's was perfect: proper confirmation, proper timing.
- Earlier entry = better — but NEVER without confirmation; that's a gamble ("tukka") raising opposing-move probability.
- **Inside the candle:** no entry question before the candle forms. Entry can build at CLOSING time — when the candle has consumed enough and closes near the extreme. Mid-candle entries aren't confirmations; unconfirmed entries get run over when price just extends. Take the entry DURING the momentum pull so momentum works in your favor.
- **Missed the candle?** A retracement/lower-level entry still keeps you safe: SL below lows + divergence shows if weakness persists.
- **Never short the absolute low:** bad-probability execution; take the pullback — options give near-support price there.

### Self-testing & the funnel
Test yourself post-session: take 10 entries; if MOST hit SL with no post-entry momentum, entries are wrong. Correct behavior: 5–6 instant points (140→150/155 same candle) = >1:1 pulled within the FIRST candle; retrace/sideways then harmless. By the 1:2 zone, SL sitting 12 points away leaves a **24-point funnel to play with — safety compounds**.

**Most important point:** small-target/small-ratio/small-SL systems REQUIRE compulsory post-entry momentum. Missing it = wrong entry: late entries miss momentum entirely; sideways-phase entries hit SL maximum-times. Somewhat EARLY is acceptable — ratio is better, 1–2 stop-outs stay manageable.

## Confidence — rule #1

No hasty entries. Nervousness or missing confirmations → don't take that entry, or don't trade the session at all. **First rule: no confidence → no execution.** With confidence there's no nervousness, no emotion; lack of confidence exposes a weak concept/understanding somewhere.

Confidence sources: ratios right, SL right, entry price right, overview right, probability known. Don't think ONE trade — think **10 trades**: same execution repeated gives some 1:3s and 2–4 SLs, so nothing today carries lasting impact.

No confidence → skip, or slash quantity (1/2/5 lots). Most dangerous combo: **no confidence + heavy quantity** → emotion → fear → collapse. Old saying fits markets perfectly: *"whoever feared is dead."* If you fear this field, it isn't for you. Everyone — including him — started with emotions and "let this one pass, I'll never repeat it" thinking.

Home distractions (kids, calls) break execution. Environment must be PEACEFUL — closed room; best if nobody even comes and goes.

**Equipment:** one script → one laptop/PC suffices. Mobile NOT for execution (price-checking fine). No fancy multi-screen rigs unless genuinely running multiple books simultaneously. Peace + confidence first; general execution rules after those. Proper entry not forming? Let the next view/combination form and execute then.

## Stop-loss rules

### Average-size SL → automatically realistic 1:3
SL should be **average candle size** — that makes the **1:3 target** a general realistic expectation (Indian markets make it realistic automatically).

### Oversized-SL exception
Low too far away (12–13–15 point SL)? Target turns unrealistic. Place the SL **slightly above the low, slightly bigger than average size** instead. Simple general rule.

### Management after entry — one rule only
ALL analysis happens BEFORE entry. After entry: NO analysis — changing-overview feelings, bounces off resistance, breaking news — none may disturb you. Once entered, end analysis right there. Remaining tasks: average-size SL near/slightly below lows (swing lows checked; extend slightly if points are few).

Single permitted intervention: approached a REALISTIC 1:3 — beyond roughly **1:2.2 up to ~1:2.5**, missing 1:3 by merely **5–7 points** — then reverses sharply back toward cost → MAY exit at break-even/cost-to-cost (or touch-negative) rather than ride to full SL. Otherwise: target → book; SL → exit.

## Backtesting walkthroughs — Part 1 charts

### Unbiased-day checklist & standard first-swing rule
Recent day answered as checklist: ATH? yes. Retested? yes. Support? yes. Bounce → resistance → sell-off? yes ⇒ indecision market, no bias. Prior instruction: high side = strength/buy; low side = weakness/sell. Difference vs today: THAT day opened into highs; today lows broke.

**Standard rule for bias-less days:** previous day gave no clarity → NEXT DAY take direction of the FIRST swing that forms — verified across three consecutive days.

### 27th Feb
Opened into higher levels; buying overview. Applied: **no entry before 9:45** regardless; first swing buying → observe pattern break; bias confirmed → buy planned (deep fall unexpected at highs). Market retraced, built high-level support; continuation AT support allows direct buy, otherwise pattern-break entry. It dropped toward lows — valid lows-break confirmation — but the SELL was deliberately SKIPPED: bias had flipped to buying-swing regime. **Bias overrides pattern.** (Entry exists in a public YouTube video.)

Adjacent support/divergence day: simple W-pattern continuation trade available; divergence appeared; bounces offered entries en route to the intraday high. Two higher-low entry flavors: usual pace-of-momentum-break entry vs simple continuation on a proper pattern — enterable on the reversal candle closing with strength WITHOUT waiting for the high break; "proper breakout" not required.

### Swing reclassification & two pattern-break types
Buying swing VOIDED once lows break **3–4 consecutive candles** — whole leg reclassified selling; buy setups ignored till fresh valid break. Two observation points mark a break: (1) **number of candles** — several push highs, lower high forms, lows start breaking; (2) **domination** — single aggressive candle consumes the previous one (large body), most significant when FIRST occurrence inside the swing (e.g., 9:45 red consuming the ENTIRE prior green when no red had consumed anything = selling-side confirmation).

### Against-flow menu & the system's two entry methods
Flow-favorable (ATH + strong buying): **ONE candle of support suffices** for buys. Against flow: EITHER a **lower high** forms → execute, OR **double-domination**: buyer dominates → seller dominates → buyer → seller again; execute ON THE SECOND SELLER DOMINATION. Gap-down handling: buying swing created after gap-down still bought per flow (support → pattern break → buy).

Two methods overall: (1) flow-favorable — S/R forms with flow → direct pattern-break execution; (2) against flow — rejection counting (two rejections, third = entry) or the double-domination trigger.

**Momentum-miss principle:** against flow without clean candle-wise confirmation, deliberately LET momentum miss; wait proper confirmation; guard against price flipping back above highs — letting lows break first yields the higher-probability execution.

### Timing law & historical universality
Whatever the first swing (buy/sell), it forms **around 10 o'clock** — S/R creation lands ~9:45–10:00 in maximum cases, verifiable years back. Minimum expectations: intraday LOW breakdown (shorts) / HIGH breakout (longs); many against-flow trades deliver it too. Live proof: random scroll back to early 2023 (Nifty ~17,000–18,000) finding mirrored setups — weekly-high/all-time-high contexts always resolve CONTINUATION-or-REVERSAL via observation. Stocks identical ("name any stock"). Selling-swing exhaustion heuristic: after gap-up from highs, counter selling swing lasts ~5/8/10 candles, then support.

### Mirror selling-flow universe
Opposite case: 3–6 days selling flow → sideways close at lows (weekly/all-time low) → next day creates BUYING swing ⇒ reversal case; minimum expectation = HIGH breakout AND continuation. Support formed off just TWO candles — identical logic mirrored.

### Favorable-buy replay & best-opportunity doctrine
Template: continuous prior-day high breakouts → gap up → morning retrace → EXPECT support at 9:45–10 (printed at the 9:45 candle). Sequence: first red, sellers push again, pullback candle, down again — THIRD candle's size = confirmation; exceptional-strength domination at the selling swing's 9:45; post-support entire wick reversed, highs kept breaking → single-candle entry legitimate, SL below.

Best opportunity in a buying market at ATHs: a dip giving LOWER LEVELS to buy — regardless of which swing delivers the dip; identify where retracement is "enough" (continuous falling low-probability) → buy support + pattern break. Superior variant: **retracement completed the PREVIOUS day + fall resumes next morning** — no better case exists. First-candle neglect: dip rejecting fully within 1–2 open candles → neglect that candle for structure.

### Classification mini-case
Green candles then support breaking near 9:45 ⇒ label SELLING swing — but do NOT auto-short: flow buying, no lower high, prior close at highs, big gap-down ALREADY counts as the retracement. Selling skipped.

### Student-guidance block
Small SL at support ⇒ occasionally **1–2 stop-outs happen — accept them; the ratio pays**. Beginners: MAX two entries, both ONLY inside the same swing and only because support identification was WRONG first time — never chain across different swings; opportunistic cut-and-re-enter not advised initially. Re-entry economics: options-buying charges negligible — exit-and-reenter costs nothing material; first SL spent → new support → fresh pace-break entry. Warning: sometimes the support sustains and bounces without you — requires being VERY quick; hence discouraged for beginners ("we prefer peace over executions").

## Swing trading — same concept, changed timeframe

"Do nothing — just change the time frame."

- Identify trend/major flow on the **DAILY** chart (moving average optional — any trend tool works; nothing else needed); locate supports there.
- Drop to **HOURLY** to find where a significant/major retracement completed.
- Execute in the intraday session using the IDENTICAL method (strength buys at lower levels); HOLD the same support-based entry for days.
- Personal preference: swings of **3, 4, 5 days**. BTST/swing/positional all the same concept; positional trend ID on the daily frame. For BTST, an "enough" bounce suffices; max cases exit via trailing.

**Entries are selective:** swing entries can't be taken daily — WAIT for the setups. Trade only sessions whose hourly frame shows significant retracement completed (or buy only after deep falls) → probability rises, clarity remains ("only buy").

**Never chase highs:** explicitly not recommended to buy swings at high levels — let a significant retracement come, let support form, then go intraday into that session. Buying-top detail: after proper retracement, buy when lows intact + higher low forming + higher highs above it + resistance breaking.

**Worked examples:**
- **21st–22nd December:** pre-fixed buying bias; entered on N/M-pattern confirmation (or selling-swing completion); HELD 21st → ~29th; exit via TRAILING (fixed ratios equally valid).
- **21st December 5-minute walkthrough, two entry options:** A (safe side, preferred): support + pattern break → smallest SL. B (if A absent): domination confirmation — lows made, 2–3 selling candles carry price back near highs (domination done; market had fallen FROM highs originally), buyers push from lows upward = confirmation → execute; SL slightly above intraday low; HOLD and trail.
- **18th–19th June gaps:** fell, gapped DOWN 18th, gapped UP 19th and ran — trailing on each new INTRADAY low (major-low trailing equally valid).
- Multi-day trail narrative: Day 2 trail; Day 3 negative → wait; non-sustaining gap-up → falling → keep trailing "till somewhere, then cut."
- Daily-chart circles marked candidate sessions (session followed by significant retracement). Where a retracement ENDS is unknowable in advance — observe that a significant one occurred, react to support formation. Big-frame law: price never falls continuously; oversized selling swings can't keep printing without bounces.
- Objection answered: "support's already formed, I'll buy the 12th" — costs exactly ONE stop-loss; correct path = wait for FRESH support identification after the deeper leg.

**Small-SL swing math:** small SLs at 1:3 reward tolerate 2–3 stop-outs — long holds, targets dwarf risk. Works best with small quantity + no fear of holding.

## Strike price selection (full revision)

- Default: **ATM** (example: 22,400) or nearest ITM within **100 points**.
- Beginners: avoid over-analyzing — more checks = more confusion; premium differences between strikes build slowly (~20–30%).
- **Divergence check mandatory** on the chosen strike.
- **Volume-spike location:** buying at support → CALL-option spike preferred; selling → PUT-option spike.
- Preference order: **WEEKLY first, always.** Weekly: check 100 pts ITM, then 200 pts ITM. Monthly fallback: 100 pts ITM or ATM. ("Sometimes monthly?" — that's HIS flexibility; students default weekly.)
- Final chooser: strike with best volume-in-your-favor + best divergence + best strength. Three pillars: **volume, price behavior, weekly-vs-monthly**.

- "**I am a trader, not an investor.**" Pick ONE pursuit — full-time trader OR full-time investor; attempting both ends in failure. Investing exposure belongs in mutual funds or long-term/positional executions, not self stock-picking.
